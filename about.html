About EONAPP.ch

EONAPP.ch is a local-first AI workspace for operators, creators, and builders: chat-first routing, Projects, Library, Workspace, Vault persistence, calm EON City navigation, and portable signed sharing designed to run cleanly on Cloudflare Pages.

What we are actually building

EONAPP is a focused operator workspace: start with EONBOT, route work into Projects, Library, Workspace, Vault, Trade Lab, Market, or EON City, and keep the product honest about which actions are local, preview-only, approved, or unavailable.

Campaign decision gate

Paid EONAPP subscriptions are available through Dodo Payments hosted checkout. Ordinary display advertising is disabled across EONAPP, including EON City. Signed-in users may voluntarily open the separate Sponsor Terminal for rewarded video; Sponsor Keys are consumable, non-cash credits for bounded temporary feature access and never come from ordinary impressions or clicks. Offerwalls, revenue sharing, cash payouts and wallet payments remain disabled. Verified referrals may separately earn non-cash referral EONKEYS for selected individual feature or limit unlocks; neither reward system grants a free subscription tier or payment credit.

  • Never gate the first result behind a campaign.
  • Do not promise earnings, credits, unlocks, or profit from sharing.
  • Any future ad, offer, payout or additional payment provider must pass legal, fraud, callback, reversal, disclosure, preview and device evidence before activation.

Local-first user systems

The EON Vault is the honest browser-side identity layer. It stores local preferences, saved work, selected profile metadata, device-bound keys, and tool history with optional encrypted export/import. Cloudflare deployments must not erase same-profile browser data.

Portable signed sharing without a link registry

New invite links use eon2 and safe Realm identity links use eon3. Each link carries its own signature and a fresh 128-bit share ID, so it verifies locally without a short-link registry, click tracker, central relationship graph, token, or active reward program.

Content and product education

The useful content lanes are practical tool explainers, local-AI setup, safe Vault backup, Projects and Library workflows, EON City navigation, and clear product-status notes. Every article should lead users to a truthful native surface, not an unverified campaign.

EONBOT assistant

EONBOT starts as a local-first guide and can route into user-provided AI providers or local runtimes. The honest product promise is control: keep Vault state local, approve automations before external action, and use Cloudflare only where a later verified relationship or security boundary genuinely needs it.
