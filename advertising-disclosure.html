Advertising & Sponsorship Disclosure

How EONAPP separates editorial content, AdSense, Sponsored EONBOT, rewarded experiences and future commercial relationships.

Effective 30 August 2026

Current state

EONAPP has Google AdSense ownership verification in source, but the AdSense account ownership marker and approval-ready Auto Ads bootstrap are present only on reviewed public guide pages. Ad serving still depends on Google site approval and live account settings. Ordinary banner/display advertising remains disabled inside EONAPP work surfaces and EON City gameplay.

Public guide advertising

Infolinks publisher code may run only on reviewed public guide pages for contextual in-text advertising and site approval/monetization testing. It is not loaded on private EONBOT, Local AI, BYOK, Vault, billing or workspace surfaces. Google AdSense, if approved and the required consent configuration is active, may also serve on reviewed public guide pages. The release policy keeps app, account, Local AI work surfaces and EON City gameplay outside the ordinary display-ad lane. Interactive guide tools and EONBOT CTA regions carry stable exclusion markers so the AdSense Auto Ads preview can keep in-page placements away from calculators and action controls. EONAPP will not ask readers to click ads, reward ad clicks or disguise ads as product controls.

Sponsored EONBOT

Hosted Sponsored EONBOT is a separate AI route. A normal AI answer and a sponsored commercial result must remain distinguishable. A sponsor should not secretly determine the assistant’s substantive answer. Eligibility can depend on country, account state, abuse controls, model economics and provider evidence.

Local AI and BYOK

Core Local AI and BYOK conversations remain separate from Sponsored AI. They are not silently forwarded through Vexrail. Sponsored Discovery is a separate explicit review-and-confirm action. It sends only the minimal intent the user approves through a same-origin server route, never the full Local AI/BYOK conversation, private memory or provider keys, and it labels sponsored output separately.

Intent and direct-link partners

Zyntent is an intent-monetization provider designed to return product, deal or merchant ads from a reviewed commercial query. EONAPP may test it only as a separate Sponsored Discovery provider after server credentials, consent and live response handling are verified; private Local AI/BYOK history, answers, memory and provider keys are not part of that request. Adsterra, Monetag and BidVertiser SmartLinks/direct links, if tested later, must appear only as clearly labelled user-initiated sponsored links. EONAPP does not automatically convert arbitrary words in an AI answer into SmartLinks and does not enable pop-under, push, injection or forced-redirect formats in the launch policy.

Rewarded video

Rewarded experiences are voluntary and separate from ordinary content advertising. Rewards are non-cash, non-transferable EONAPP feature credits. A browser-side “video complete” signal alone should not be treated as sufficient authority for valuable permanent rewards when stronger provider verification is available.

Affiliate and direct commercial relationships

If EONAPP later receives a commission, placement fee, free product, sponsorship or another material benefit connected to a recommendation, the relationship should be disclosed clearly near the relevant commercial content. Editorial pages should still explain meaningful trade-offs and should not claim a product is best solely because it pays more.

Paid acquisition

EONAPP may advertise its own guides through native, push or other paid traffic sources. Paid acquisition does not change the requirement that the landing page deliver the content promised in the creative. Traffic economics are measured separately from ad clicks: visitor cost, engagement, EONBOT usage, AI cost, sponsor/reward revenue and subscriptions all contribute to the decision to scale.
