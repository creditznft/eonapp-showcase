Editorial Policy

How EONAPP researches, reviews, updates and separates editorial guidance from advertising and sponsored recommendations.

Effective 29 August 2026

Purpose

EONAPP publishes guides to help readers make practical decisions about AI software, local AI hardware, APIs, privacy, workflows and EONAPP features. Search visibility and advertising can help fund that work, but an article must remain useful without an ad click, sponsored result or EONAPP account.

Original value requirement

We do not publish a page merely because a keyword variation exists. New guides should add original explanation, first-party testing, a calculator, checklist, decision framework, screenshot, benchmark, source synthesis or another practical contribution. We avoid mass-producing near-duplicate AI pages whose main purpose is ranking manipulation.

Sources and changing facts

Provider prices, model names, policies and product capabilities can change quickly. When a guide depends on those facts, we prefer primary provider documentation, show an update/review date and link readers to the source. We distinguish measured EONAPP observations from estimates and general guidance.

AI-assisted drafting

AI tools may assist research organisation, outlining, editing and drafting, but publication responsibility remains with EONAPP. A generated statement is not treated as evidence. Changing factual claims should be checked against appropriate sources before publication.

Corrections and updates

When a material factual error is found, the page should be corrected rather than preserved for search performance. Time-sensitive comparison pages should be periodically reviewed or marked when data may be stale. A retired guide may be redirected only when the replacement genuinely answers the same user need.

Advertising separation

Advertising does not decide the substantive recommendation in an editorial guide. Google ads, if AdSense approval is granted and units are later activated, must be visually separate from navigation, EONBOT controls, calculators and other interactive elements. Sponsored recommendations inside EONAPP are separately labelled and governed by their own eligibility and privacy rules.

EONBOT handoffs

Guide CTAs may prepare a draft question for EONBOT, but the user should be able to review and edit it before sending. We do not manufacture AI requests merely to create sponsored inventory.

Commercial-intent topics

We deliberately cover topics with real purchasing and deployment intent—AI APIs, hardware, cloud services, privacy tooling and business software—because those are relevant to EONAPP users and can support sustainable advertising. Commercial value does not justify irrelevant topics, deceptive headlines, keyword stuffing or thin pages.
