## Mission

Build a truthful productive mission loop that turns Command District orientation into real, reviewable EONAPP outcomes. A mission is guidance around existing product surfaces—not a simulated job, fake success screen or hidden automation.

## Required mission families

1. First-run orientation and safe City controls.
2. Create or resume a private project.
3. Configure Local AI or a user-owned direct BYOK rail with explicit privacy labels.
4. Prepare an image/video creator proposal or guide artifact without claiming generation when no runtime/provider is available.
5. Prepare or review an automation proposal without inventing a queue or job.
6. Review Vault backup/recovery readiness without claiming cloud sync or restore unless proven.

## Outcome contract

Every completed mission must point to a real persisted or reviewable outcome outside City, such as:

- a saved project shell;
- a local setting or explicit setup receipt;
- a saved guide/storyboard/artifact;
- a reviewable automation proposal;
- a backup/recovery readiness receipt;
- a canonical route with an honest blocked/pending state.

No mission may award money, tokens, subscription discounts, cash-equivalent value, fabricated EONKEYS or speculative ownership. Non-financial local reputation, learning milestones and cosmetics may remain presentation-only. EONKEY-compatible feature unlocks stay blocked until their server lifecycle is genuinely proven.
