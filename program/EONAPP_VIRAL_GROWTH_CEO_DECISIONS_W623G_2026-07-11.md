# W623G CEO Decisions — Viral Growth Without Fake Rewards

Date: 2026-07-11  
Status: source decision frozen; referral reward programme remains proof-gated

## The growth thesis

EONAPP should grow because users can turn useful work into attractive, public-safe things worth passing on—not because the product promises money, unlimited AI, fake scarcity or rewards for unverified clicks.

The viral engine has four loops:

1. **Invite loop** — share a signed EONAPP or EON City link.
2. **Creation loop** — hand a user-selected local image or video to the device share menu after review.
3. **Progress loop** — create a branded public-safe card for a creation, project milestone, City mission or Vault Reveal.
4. **Campaign loop** — ask EONBOT to prepare captions, visual direction, channel variants and a manual posting plan.

## Frozen CEO decisions

### Launch now

- A top-right Share action on every normal app surface, with native Chat and City ownership preserved.
- One Share Command Center rather than unrelated sharing panels.
- Native local image/video handoff where the browser/OS supports file sharing.
- Copy-caption and manual-upload fallback where native file sharing is unavailable.
- Branded 4:5 PNG cards generated locally for Creation, Project, City and Vault Reveal milestones.
- Manual destination handoffs for WhatsApp, Telegram, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Reddit and email.
- EONBOT campaign drafts that remain review-first and never post automatically.
- Clear privacy boundaries: private chats, files, Vault secrets, provider keys and hidden project data are excluded.

### Do not launch yet

- No EONKEY for clicking, sharing, posting, signing up or purchasing until the W629 server ledger proves attribution, qualification, caps, retention, refund/dispute handling, idempotency and abuse reversal.
- No paid-ad recommendation for referral links until that same proof exists.
- No automatic social posting, connected social-account tokens, background publishing or fake posting proof.
- No cash, commission, subscription discount, renewal credit, wallet, token, provider credit or whole-tier reward.
- No claim that EONAPP can measure virality while privacy-safe measurement remains unimplemented.

## Viral score

The W623G scoring contract gives the implemented source **7.2/10**.

Implemented points:

- universal shell Share access;
- signed public links;
- local native-file handoff;
- creator output handoffs;
- branded progress cards;
- EONBOT campaign drafts;
- platform variants;
- clear disclosure/guardrails.

Remaining 2.8 points:

- server-authoritative attribution;
- qualified EONKEY grant ledger;
- refund/dispute/abuse reversal;
- privacy-safe measurement.

A 10/10 claim is prohibited until all four blockers have real evidence.

## Paid promotion decision

Users may organically share creations and ordinary invite links now. EONAPP must not tell users to spend money advertising a reward link while rewards, attribution and reversal are inactive. After W629 passes, a limited paid-promotion lane may be reconsidered with:

- clear material-benefit disclosure in the ad/post itself;
- no brand bidding, cloaking, cookie stuffing, spam or misleading earnings claims;
- capped, non-cash EONKEY rewards only;
- server idempotency and self-referral rejection;
- hold/reversal rules for refunds, disputes and abuse;
- channel-specific terms and support escalation.

## Product principle

The strongest long-term loop is **creation → visible result → beautiful public-safe card/file → one-tap handoff → recipient starts creating**. Referral rewards are an accelerator after proof, not the product’s reason to exist.
