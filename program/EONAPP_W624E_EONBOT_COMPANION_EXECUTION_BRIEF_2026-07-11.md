## Mission

Replace the current companion placeholder behavior with one useful, dismissible EONBOT Orbit companion inside the existing Command District. It must guide the user toward real routes and saved work without pretending to be an autonomous live agent.

## Required implementation

1. Create a distinct EONBOT Orbit silhouette consistent with Productive Nocturne and readable beside the Wayfinder.
2. Provide deterministic local presentation states for follow, lead, point, think, speak, scan, celebrate, warn and help.
3. Tie hints only to actual W624C destinations, current route state, visible saved-project metadata or explicit proof-gated boundaries.
4. Never invent jobs, agent progress, project success, payments, rewards or operational status.
5. Keep microphone and speech disabled until explicit user action; captions remain primary.
6. Provide visible mute, dismiss, reduced-motion and “show less guidance” controls.
7. Prevent the companion from blocking paths, camera sightlines, interaction cards, spawn or Unstuck recoveries.
8. Preserve W624D camera profiles and Wayfinder controls without hidden auto-navigation.
9. Keep all companion state local and disposable; do not mutate projects, providers, billing, referrals, Vault or account data.
10. Preserve W624B runtime ownership, disposal, session-expiry and re-entry behavior.

## Acceptance

Produce focused source gates/tests plus real-browser evidence where available for:

- all nine companion presentation states;
- useful non-repeating hints on the first-sixty-second route;
- mute, dismiss and reduced-motion behavior;
- no path/camera/input obstruction;
- no fake operational claims or private-data exposure;
- clean disposal and re-entry;
- compatibility with W624D Wayfinder/camera and the deterministic Codex predeploy suite.

Do not claim live AI conversation, autonomous agent execution, physical-device quality or final character art from source tests.
