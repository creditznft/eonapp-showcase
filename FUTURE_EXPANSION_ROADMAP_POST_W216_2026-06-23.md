# Future Expansion Roadmap After W216

## Product principle

Keep the current product simple, local-first, and evidence-led. Do not reactivate commercial, payout, reward, provider, exchange, or payment behavior until its own legal, fraud, security, and delivery proof is complete.

## F1 — Referral trust and fraud-control design (before any incentive)

- Bind a qualified referral relationship only after authenticated account creation and a clearly defined qualifying action.
- Reject self-referrals, duplicate household/device patterns, replayed payloads, and suspicious conversion loops.
- Store only a pseudonymous relationship record in the existing referral-tree database; never store raw portable links or a click log by default.
- Define a dispute/reversal process and a privacy notice before any value is attached.
- Keep `eon2` and `eon3` as durable share formats. A new link should create a new share ID, not replace the stable account/Realm identity.

## F2 — Realm Studio depth without central link dependence

- Add local templates: calm workspace, gallery, study room, research lab, creator portfolio.
- Add versioned local Realm snapshots and encrypted export/import.
- Add optional user-signed visual metadata, without making a public central Realm registry mandatory.
- Add accessible preview cards and offline-safe 2D representations.
- Treat public publishing as a separate evidence-backed feature; do not imply that a local Realm record is a hosted world.

## F3 — EON City visual direction and performance

- Commission or generate a small, cohesive visual asset kit rather than layering more effects.
- Add visual regression screenshots on a controlled browser runner.
- Build a performance budget: first interaction, memory, reduced-motion, 4 GB Android, and no-WebGL fallback thresholds.
- Keep 2D as default. Gate 3D behind capability, user choice, reduced-motion, battery/save-data checks, and an immediate return path.
- Add one polished “hero tour” only after real screenshots establish baseline quality.

## F4 — Data survival and account boundary

- Run cloud-update persistence proof with before/after snapshots.
- Test encrypted Vault backup/restore on a physical device.
- Document storage schema migrations, backup compatibility, and rollback behavior.
- Add explicit account sync only after privacy, recovery, encryption, and threat-model review.

## F5 — Market and catalog authenticity

- Keep current local generated preview behavior honest.
- Add a real catalog only when inventory, pricing, checkout, receipt, refund, support, and ownership wording can be verified end-to-end.
- Never present a generated local preview as a minted NFT, tradeable asset, or paid entitlement without proof.

## F6 — Trade research maturity

- Keep all current flows research/paper-only.
- Add educational scenarios, saved watchlists, and exportable research notes first.
- Consider live data or connectors only after compliance review, credential boundary design, consent, rate limits, audit logs, and kill switches.
- Do not connect exchange execution or withdrawals until an independent security review is complete.

## F7 — Observability and support

- Retain redacted, local-first diagnostics.
- Define a consented incident-report workflow with no secrets, no raw URLs, and no hidden third-party telemetry.
- Add a minimal support issue export that users can inspect before sharing.

## F8 — Monetization decision wave (future only)

- Choose a single campaign/payout model only after written provider approval, callback verification, fraud controls, legal terms, support procedures, refund/reversal rules, and a Preview evidence run.
- Do not mix ads, offerwalls, subscriptions, referral rewards, revenue share, and marketplace commerce in one activation.
- Start with a small, reviewable, opt-in pilot; keep a global kill switch and clear user-facing status.
