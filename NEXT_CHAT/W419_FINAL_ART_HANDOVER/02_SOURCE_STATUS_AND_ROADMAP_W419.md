## Mandatory external proof before stronger claims

1. **City visual proof:** desktop, Android and iOS screenshots/video; keyboard, mouse, touch, portrait/landscape, safe areas, reduced motion, pause/reset and Command Deck.
2. **Google OAuth:** production testing mode using a disposable approved Google account; callback, refresh, sign-out and deletion behavior recorded without credentials.
3. **Sync Basic:** provision dedicated D1 binding; two-device opt-in, migration/import, offline edits, conflict copy, tombstone propagation, sign-out and empty-device restore.
4. **Final binary City art:** source/commission original or licensed assets; W417 preflight, human art/licence review, SHA-256, same-origin paths, GLB, KTX2/Basis, LOD0/1/2, quality budgets and target-device evidence.
