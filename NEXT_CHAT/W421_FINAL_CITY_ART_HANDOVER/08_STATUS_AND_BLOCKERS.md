# W421 Status and Blocking Evidence

## Source status

W421 is code-complete for the original source-art and composition scope. The canonical Babylon City now contains PBR procedural geometry, an original local vector-art system, local color/atmosphere profiles and user-visible art review compositions.

## What blocks a final institutional-grade visual claim

1. Reviewed original/commissioned/licensed binary 3D art.
2. W417 asset release preflight: provenance, SHA-256, GLB, KTX2/Basis, LODs and budget metrics.
3. Human art/rights review.
4. Desktop, Android and iOS screenshots/video plus actual performance observations after final art is loaded.

## Other activation blockers

- real production Google OAuth proof;
- dedicated D1 provisioning and W412 two-device Sync proof;
- any decision to activate social posting, payments, rewards or user deployment.

No source-only change can truthfully replace those proofs.
