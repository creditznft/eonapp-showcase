- lean runnable source and `public/` source assets required by the build;
- W419 original SVG art kit and W420/W421 composition/review code;
- all relevant gates, unit tests, docs and handover records;

- art inventory and contact sheet;
- Codex execution/deploy runbook;

- `node_modules`, `dist`, artifacts, report caches, browser profiles and generated test output;
- `.env`, secret values, OAuth/session keys, Cloudflare/D1/R2 exports and user/customer data;
- nested ZIPs/old handover archives;
- unreviewed final GLB/KTX2/audio/animation binaries;
- unverified real-device screenshots or videos.
