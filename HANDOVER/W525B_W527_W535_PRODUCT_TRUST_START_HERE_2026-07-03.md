# W525B–W535 — Product, Continuity and Trust Start Here

## What is now in the current source

This source expands the W525A continuity baseline through a clean Account, Profile, Settings and Vault experience; safe local evidence lanes; an isolated Trust Hub package; explicit cross-origin Capsule guidance; and historical-document cleanup.

The active EONAPP product truth remains:

- `eonapp.ch` is the only canonical daily app origin.
- A user can export/import one encrypted Portable Workspace Capsule containing every **eligible** local workspace record together.
- Capsule is manual, user-held, and user-confirmed. It is not automatic cross-device sync, cloud recovery, account recreation, or compression.
- Google Login is identity-only. The Google Drive connector is approved product direction but remains disabled and disconnected until its separate storage-consent/evidence lane is completed.
- Vault Reveals are visual collection cards only. They do not mint, transfer, trade, pay, create wallet ownership, or grant rewards.
- EON.HUB is a separate static public Trust & Rescue package; it is not an EONAPP app/storage/login/OAuth/payment origin.

## Product architecture

The user-facing structure is deliberately simple:

1. **Account/Profile** — identity, preferences, notification and support entrypoints.
2. **Settings** — grouped general, appearance, voice/language, data/recovery, privacy/safety, local AI, connections and billing controls.
3. **Vault Overview** — a plain-language recovery status and the next safe action.
4. **Recovery** — create, download, inspect and import the encrypted Capsule.
5. **Backup** — manual device/file handoff now; future Google Drive encrypted-snapshot path clearly marked `planned—not connected`.
6. **AI keys** — advanced-only, separate from backups.
7. **Vault Reveals** — visual cards, separate from safety or recovery.
8. **Safety** — privacy, permissions and product-truth information.

The shell’s account popover opens on a delayed desktop fine-pointer hover and still supports click, touch and keyboard interaction. It does not force hover behavior on phones/tablets.
