### Station interaction rules

- Structure click: identifies the destination and opens its maintained surface.
- Terminal click: plays a stationary interaction/work clip and opens the same surface.
- NPC click: suspends local walking, plays a stationary talk/gesture clip and opens the same surface.
- Keyboard/menu actions require explicit user action and use the same authority.
- A walk clip is legal only while the NPC anchor is measurably changing position.
- Reduced-motion mode keeps station staff stationary without disabling their work surface.
- No station may navigate to a missing page or imply background work that did not occur.
