# W420 — EON City Cinematic Art Direction

W420 adds an original local rendering-composition layer to the canonical Babylon City. It is a color-and-atmosphere profile, not an external LUT, texture download or final visual certification.

## Profiles

- **Lite:** stable, legible fallback with no vignette or dithering.
- **Balanced:** local ACES tone mapping, restrained vignette, no screen dithering/grain, and open readable landmarks.
- **Cinematic:** local ACES tone mapping, open readable contrast, bounded vignette, no screen dithering/grain, and the existing cinematic-only shadow path.

## Safety and honesty

- no remote LUT or color-grade texture;
- no user data;
- this is not final binary art and does not claim final art approval;
- no device performance or final flagship-art claim.

Real-device visual proof and W417-approved GLB/KTX2 art remain required before any institutional-grade final visual claim.
