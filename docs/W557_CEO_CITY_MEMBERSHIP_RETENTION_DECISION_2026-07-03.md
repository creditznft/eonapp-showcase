# W557 — CEO City Membership and Retention Decision

**Status:** source-controlled product decision; no checkout, paid access, email delivery, browser push, social messaging, preview deployment or production deployment is activated by this wave.

## 1. City access remains identity-gated

Full EON City play remains behind the existing EONAPP Google/EON identity session. Guest pages remain lightweight and do not import the heavy City runtime. The identity session is not a Drive grant, cloud backup, local-model permission, newsletter enrolment, email-delivery permission, browser-push permission or social-message permission.

## 2. Monetisation decision

**Subscriptions are the only planned EON City monetisation.** The City must not use advertising, rewarded unlocks, lootbox pressure, coins, referral income, pay-to-progress progression, or a fake “upgrade” wall.

The intended membership ladder uses the existing individual-only planning contract:

| Tier | City destination | Honest value boundary | Current availability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EON Free | Arrival & Command District | Core City, local work, Project Console, City Lite, accessibility and recovery remain useful. | Core access |
| EON Plus | Signal Lounge | Maintained Plus Pack library and versioned professional workroom updates. | Planned, not for sale |
| EON Studio | Creator Atrium Studio Wing | Maintained solo creator, business and research operating-system workrooms. | Planned, not for sale |
| EON Power | Strategy Observatory | Current non-Signature individual Pack library and cross-workroom systems. | Planned, not for sale |
| EON Max | Horizon Key Gallery | Future individual all-access catalogue and explicitly labelled stable-preview Pack stream. | Designed, not for sale |

A future paid City district is only legitimate after all of these exist:

1. The maintained workroom/Pack catalogue exists and is browser/mobile reviewed.
2. The tier is server-verified. Browser storage cannot be a paid entitlement authority.
3. Checkout, renewal, cancellation, refund/reversal and support flows are tested with the selected provider.
4. Public terms, privacy/support wording and local consumer-law review are complete.
5. The exact inclusion remains accurate. No “unlimited hosted AI,” cloud-storage, team, enterprise or priority-human-support claim may be implied.

Free users must never be artificially blocked from ordinary local work, exports, backup guidance, safety controls, basic Project records, City Lite or accessibility/performance recovery.

## 3. W557 implementation

- The Project Console opens as a same-tab foreground Workroom.
- City captures the current exploration pose, pauses while the console is open, and restores location/camera on return.
- Browser pointer lock is never forged after return; the person must choose it again.
- A planning-only Membership map is visible inside City. It offers no price, checkout, paid action or entitlement change.
- W557 never reads or writes a payment state in browser storage.

## 4. Retention and notification decision

### Released now

- Local Activity Center: small redacted in-app updates, category preferences and quiet-hours preference.
- A clear in-product statement that Google sign-in does not enrol the user in email, browser push, a newsletter or social messages.

### Not released now

- Browser push permission or subscription.
- Email service, newsletter, campaign, mailing list or login-triggered email.
- Social direct messages or social-network notification automation.
- AI/EONBOT outward sending.

### Required future delivery contract

Every outbound channel must be separately built and released:

- **Service email:** operational notices only, purpose-bound and independently documented.
- **Product/newsletter email:** an explicit, granular optional choice; a visible unsubscribe/stop path; consent/revocation/version/timestamp recorded server-side only once the delivery system exists.
- **Browser push:** a contextual explicit action—not a first-load prompt—with category preferences, quiet hours, revocation and endpoint lifecycle proof.
- **Connected social:** only after explicit account connection, channel selection and final user review. Never auto-DM contacts or post/substitute on the user’s behalf.
- **EONBOT:** may propose a local reminder but never send email, push or social messaging without the released channel and explicit user choice.
