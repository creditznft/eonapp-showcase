# W369 — EON City Adaptive Soundscape

## Delivered source boundary

W369 adds a local, default-off adaptive soundscape controller for Immersive Work Mode and Spatial Command Space. It permits a very quiet procedural ambience bed and short UI cues only after a person explicitly enables the settings and activates a control. The controller disposes nodes and the local audio context when a City renderer exits.

## Explicit limits

No original music stems, voice pack, microphone capture, downloaded audio, remote audio transport, telemetry, AI narration, automatic speech, or automatic audio is shipped in this code-only wave. The Music control records a preference and honestly reports that original stems are not packaged. The Voice control is captions-first only; it does not speak.

## Accessibility and sensory rules

- All sound controls are default-off and remain optional.
- Reduced Sensory disables ambience and UI cues without changing City progress.
- Visual status remains available when all audio is off or unavailable.
- Audio begins only inside a user action path; loading a City surface never activates it.
- Later evidence must test volume, mute, screen readers, reduced motion, low-end mobile devices and browser autoplay policies.

## Not a proof claim

W369 does not claim final music quality, final voice quality, real-device listening proof, browser compatibility proof, accessibility certification, or production deployment.
