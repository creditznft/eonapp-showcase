# W667B — Deterministic-Infinite Expanse

## CEO decision

The Expanse must not be a random-noise maze or a repeated 3×3 demonstration. It is a seed-stable, streamed open world outside the authored EONCITY Core.

## Active contract

- The authored nine-district Core remains the recognisable product centre.
- The Expanse streams a 5×5 visible horizon and a 3×3 interactive neighbourhood.
- Six-by-six cell regions share a coherent district archetype, palette and atmosphere.
- Individual cells vary street profile, plaza, lot layout, building forms, activity, gameplay purpose and rare landmark.
- The same seed and coordinates reproduce the same place.
- A different seed produces a different map.
- Streets remain connected at every cardinal edge.
- The practical coordinate bound is 1,000,000 world units with no visible border.
- Cells recycle incrementally; there is still one Babylon scene, canvas and render loop.
- Discoveries are private by default and may be recorded in Atlas only by explicit action.

## Variety authority

The grammar includes:

- 10 coherent region archetypes;
- 10 street and plaza profiles;
- 30+ modular building forms across regional kits;
- deterministic 3–5 lot compositions;
- 8 landmark types with uncommon, rare, epic and legendary rarity;
- 10 productive or exploratory purposes;
- a million-plus declared combination space before coordinate-specific variation.

## Interaction authority

Rare landmarks are exact pick targets. Clicking a visible landmark cannot fall through to Transit or another nearby object. It opens one reviewed action only and never starts work, travel, voice, checkout or navigation automatically.

## Remaining human proof

This source checkpoint still requires a dependency-backed build and authenticated browser proof for visual quality, long-distance streaming, frame-time stability, collision, Atlas return and mobile interaction.
