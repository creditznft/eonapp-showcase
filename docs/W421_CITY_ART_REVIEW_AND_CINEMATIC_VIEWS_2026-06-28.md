# W421 — City Art Review and Cinematic Views

W421 makes the City’s actually shipped original source art inspectable in the canonical Babylon experience.

## Included

- a City Controls **Art review** panel;
- the real W419 18-piece local vector-art inventory, grouped by material, backdrop and decal use;
- the active W420 Lite, Balanced or Cinematic color-and-atmosphere profile;
- six local cinematic views: Arrival Gate, Command Deck, Creator Atrium, Forge Bay, Signal Tower and Archive Gardens;
- no new route, private-data exposure, screenshot capture, video capture, upload, device probe or remote request.

## Honest boundary

W421 is not final binary art and does not certify visual quality, device performance or institutional-grade final 3D delivery. The original vector fallback is shipped and visible. Final GLB/KTX2 art still requires the W417 provenance/hash/LOD/human-review release procedure and real-device visual proof.

## Manual review use

1. Enter `/eoncity` and open **City controls**.
2. Open **Art review**.
3. Review each quality profile and each of the six local views.
4. Use the separate Validation Lab and Device Lab to record what a person actually observed.
5. Capture screenshots/video only with the reviewer’s own browser or device tools; the City does not capture or upload them.
