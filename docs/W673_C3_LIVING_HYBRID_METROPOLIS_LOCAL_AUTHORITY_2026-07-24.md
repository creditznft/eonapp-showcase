# W673 — C3 Living Hybrid Metropolis local authority

Status: local source only. No remote branch, preview, deployment or merge.

This wave turns the approved C3 concept into a deterministic pure product plan:

- every current room is preserved as a District Sanctum
- every Sanctum receives a larger District Belt
- every Belt has purposeful streets, public work areas and secondary buildings
- every district has three to six active functional NPC anchors
- every district has a Transit Capsule-compatible station
- every district has an explicit Expanse gate recorded by Atlas
- functional objects take priority over decorative geometry
- one dominant and two secondary motions form the visual-noise budget

The existing Connected Core plan now carries this authority, but renderer and
coordinate migration will be completed in later local vertical slices after the
pure plan remains green.
