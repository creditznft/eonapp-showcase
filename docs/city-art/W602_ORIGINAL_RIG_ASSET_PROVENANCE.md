# W602 original Navigator and EONBOT rig assets

## Honest release state

The six GLB files in `assets/city/models/` are original, deterministic,
source-controlled stylised production candidates generated by
`scripts/build-w602-city-rig-assets.mjs`. They are not a claim that the City
has passed owner visual approval, browser/device visual proof, or AAA-quality
acceptance. The City retains its procedural fallback if an asset fails to load.

The W602 assets are textureless PBR candidates with articulated node hierarchies
and authored transform-animation clips. They intentionally do not claim skinning
or final mocap/natural-performance quality. A later art pass may replace them
with approved skinned rigs, KTX2/Basis texture sets, facial blend shapes, and
motion-capture or professionally authored clips after source, licence, and
visual-review gates are satisfied.

## Provenance and safeguards

- Origin: EONAPP original in-house work.
- Licence: EONAPP controlled original work.
- Third-party derivative: no.
- Remote network, user data, telemetry, audio, credentials: none.
- Generator: `scripts/build-w602-city-rig-assets.mjs`.
- Runtime: same-origin GLB only through the City asset catalog and runtime.
- KTX2/Basis: not used in W602 because these candidates contain no textures.
  KTX2 is a required later pipeline gate for texture-bearing hero revisions,
  not a label applied to textureless files.

## Animation contract

Navigator: Idle, Walk, Run, TurnLeft, TurnRight, LookAround, Inspect, Wave,
Acknowledge, Point, Celebrate, Arrival.

EONBOT: HoverIdle, Follow, Observe, Scan, Speak, Guide, Greet, Alert,
Celebrate, Return, Listen, Perch, Orbit, Acknowledge.

All animation invocation must be grounded in visible City state. EONBOT does
not claim autonomous task execution, background work, private-data access, or
voice AI availability. Voice remains explicit, captions-first, and browser
capability-gated.
