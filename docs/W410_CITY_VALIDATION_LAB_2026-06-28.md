# W410 — EON City Validation Lab

**Status:** source-only manual evidence readiness.  
**Canonical City:** Babylon at `/eoncity`.

## What W410 adds

- A visible **Validation Lab** inside City controls, intentionally separate from the existing Device Lab.
- Ten human-run cases covering arrival/wayfinding, desktop control/reset, midrange quality governor, Android and iOS touch/safe areas, reduced motion/sensory settings, district review/return, collision/readability, legacy route/cache recovery and performance record handoff.
- Explicit foreground save, local export and local clear actions for short, non-sensitive notes only.
- A direct **Open Device Lab** action for the separate performance checklist. W410 does not sample, score or grade performance itself.

## Truth and boundaries

W410 does not certify the City. It does not inspect a browser, device, GPU, screenshot, video, real touch input, service worker, deployment or production route.

No screenshot or video is uploaded, no device identifier is collected, no telemetry is sent, no case is passed automatically and no launch approval is issued. A manually completed local list is still only human-reported evidence awaiting independent review.

## Required real evidence

Collect real desktop and mobile evidence before stronger City claims:

1. Desktop standard and midrange/desktop GPU: keyboard, mouse/look, HUD focus, reset, pause/resume, Command Deck and quality-governor fallback.
2. Real Android browser: joystick, camera swipe, touch controls, safe areas, portrait/landscape and City Map fallback.
3. Real iOS Safari: the same touch, safe-area, orientation and fallback checks.
4. Clean screenshots and a short video for Arrival Gate, Mission Board, Creator Atrium and Forge Bay; no DevTools or debug overlays.
5. Legacy `/realm#my-realm-3d` route/cache proof after the W405 service-worker update.
6. Honest record of every collision, readability, visual, control or performance defect.

W406B asset provenance and binary-art quality gates remain separate. W410 does not convert the current authored-procedural City into final art evidence.
