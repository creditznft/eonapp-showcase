# RT92 Owner Visual Acceptance Checklist

This checklist is for real headed GPU/browser review after Codex applies the exact RT92 candidate. Source completion does not substitute for owner visual acceptance.

## Command Hub
- Living Nexus is the clear focal point on arrival and does not obstruct the player/camera.
- Motherboard traces, route pylons, station crests, skyline and ambient service life read as one authored environment.
- New world cards are sharp and correctly cropped at desktop and mobile widths.
- Lighting is crisp; bloom/emission never washes out structure or text.

## Signal Frontier
- Gateway Overlook, Beacon Fields, Archive Ruins, Transit Scar and Horizon Vault are visually distinguishable without relying on labels.
- Restoration visibly changes damaged/repaired art state.
- Deep-art structures do not create traversal/collision regressions.
- Environmental service life and reveal VFX appear only after Signal is actually entered.

## Storm Sector
- Command Spire, Atmospheric Stabilizer and Charged Transit Gate remain the hero landmarks.
- Industrial continuity and support set pieces eliminate empty gaps between hero locations.
- Weather/lightning remains readable and never destroys objective visibility.
- Service units and VFX suspend correctly when Storm is left.

## My Frontier
- Seven districts read as distinct city identities.
- Roads, plazas, public infrastructure, district furniture and skyline make the world feel like a city rather than isolated plots.
- Design Pavilion, Research Observatory, Expedition Hangar, Reflection Garden and Vault Reveal Gallery present their real RT92 GLBs after validated load.
- W770 truthful fallbacks remain visible if a bespoke GLB is unavailable or rejected.
- Construction and operational state remain visually clear.
- Reflection Garden provides deliberate visual calm and Vault Reveal Gallery does not resemble a store/marketplace.

## Cross-world sharpness/performance
- Lite is still attractive and complete, not an "ugly mode".
- Balanced is the default target and should remain smooth on the owner RTX 3050 laptop.
- Cinematic increases density/motion without changing progression or hiding required gameplay cues.
- Re-entering worlds is materially faster after warm cache and does not reload all art unnecessarily.
- No objective panel, menu, or world marker obscures the player or critical interaction target.
- Edge and Chrome both survive Hub → Signal → Storm → My Frontier → Hub repeated transitions without black frame, missing art or leaked animation.
