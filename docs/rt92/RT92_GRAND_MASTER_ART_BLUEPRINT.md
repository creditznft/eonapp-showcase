# RT92 Grand Master Art Blueprint — Wave 1 Authority

RT92 extends the frozen RT91 Living Frontier candidate. It does not replace the maintained Babylon runtime, gameplay progression, persistence or asset cache authorities.

## Locked visual law

Every major playable environment is authored against fifteen visible layers: hero silhouette, skyline depth, major architecture, secondary structures, ground language, vertical detail, environmental props, micro-detail, vector graphics, lighting hierarchy, atmosphere, ambient motion, population, environmental story and cinematic composition.

The shared material system uses twelve reusable families. Visual sharpness is produced primarily by silhouette, surface hierarchy, controlled exposure, restrained emission and near/mid/far separation. Neutral structure occupies at least ~70% of a normal frame; emissive material is capped at ~10% outside exceptional authored events. Giant floating paragraphs, texture-heavy architecture and obvious neighbouring asset repetition are not accepted art strategies.

## World identities

- Command Hub — technological heart; Living Nexus, motherboard floor and vertical civic technology.
- Signal Frontier — lost technological frontier; weathered relays, archive ruins, fractured transit and visible restoration.
- Storm Sector — hazardous industrial weather system; connected infrastructure, grounding systems, damaged surfaces and authored storm motion.
- My Frontier — persistent living user city; seven districts with distinct architecture, public fabric, construction and resident life.

## Delivery budget

The full RT92 programme targets ~8 MB of new GLB binary art, with a hard quality-profile ceiling no greater than 12 MB and a vector target below 300 KB. The first-frame Command Hub binary delta remains zero. Optional art is same-origin, progressively admitted and disposable outside the active/warm world rings.

## Runtime boundary

RT92 shared art policy owns no Babylon engine, scene, render loop, progression, asset fetcher or network channel. The maintained W731 runtime remains authoritative.
