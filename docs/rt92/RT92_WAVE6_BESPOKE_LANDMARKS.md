- Expedition Hangar — heavy launch shell, departure aperture, rails, gantries and expedition silhouette.

Every landmark ships LOD0/LOD1/LOD2. There are 15 GLBs total, zero external textures, and source/public copies are byte-identical and SHA-256 verified by tests.

## Safety and truth

- Existing canonical Babylon scene only; no Engine/Scene/render-loop ownership.
- Plot pads and curbs remain collision authority; detail meshes cannot create hidden traversal snags.
- Same-origin, content-addressed `/assets/city/rt92/...` paths only.
- W770 composition remains visible while a bespoke asset is queued/loading/rejected.
- W770 composition is hidden only after the bespoke asset passes load, visibility, material, bounds, target-height and grounding truth.
- A rejected bespoke load cannot blank the plot.
- Catalogue completion and runtime presented completion are reported separately.
