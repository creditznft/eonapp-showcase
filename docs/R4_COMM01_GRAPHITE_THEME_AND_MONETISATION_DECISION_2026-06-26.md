# R4-COMM-01 — Graphite Default and Monetisation Decision

**Status:** Source-planning decision only. It activates no checkout, prices,
subscription, provider connection, webhooks, paid entitlement, ads, CPA offers,
affiliate payout, referral credit, commission, or external workflow execution.

## 1. Product-default theme

**Decision: Graphite is the EONAPP default theme for new and previously
implicit profiles.** It follows the calm near-black, high-contrast visual
language used in the supplied reference: a quiet dark canvas, compact neutral
surfaces, clear white type, and small restrained accent states.

- **Graphite** is the default application shell.
- **Obsidian** and **Neon Night** remain the two explicit alternate dark themes.
- A person who had chosen Graphite, Obsidian or Neon Night explicitly keeps it.
  Earlier silent/default `classic-eon` and `system` values migrate to Graphite
  because they did not represent a current supported preference.
- Realm and City palettes remain independent local creative settings. This does
  not repaint a person’s Realm or alter their stored appearance.

## 2. CEO revenue model

EONAPP should launch as a **professional, ad-free work product**:

1. a generous free local core;
2. later, a small number of maintained membership tiers;
3. optional one-time **official outcome packs** for people who need one clear
   system without subscribing.

Do **not** sell individual prompt cards, buttons, basic exports, local user
work, recovery records, safety controls, or artificial AI-message quotas.
EONAPP is local/BYOK-first, so it must not imitate an inference-cost pricing
model it does not operate.

### Launch economics: what stays free

Free includes all active core product value:

- EONBOT foreground planning and review;
- Workspace, Projects, Library, local exports and backup guidance;
- 20 official Blueprints and the 10 approval-first local workflows;
- Apps, Workrooms, AI Crew and Insights & Forecasts;
- manual/CSV Market Intelligence, Business Intelligence, Forecast Studio,
  Research Journal and Local Data Lab;
- privacy, safety, delete and data portability controls.

### Planned catalogue — not for sale yet

| Offering | Planning price | When it can exist | What it sells |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **EON Free** | $0 reference | Active now | The usable local-first core. |
| **EON Plus / Studio / Power / Max** | Not public; each future monthly tier must remain within a **$49.99 USD reference ceiling** until a new owner decision | Only after maintained value, approval, hosted checkout, webhooks, entitlement, cancellation/refund and support lifecycle proof exist | Future maintained membership value; no hosted AI quota. |
| **Official Outcome Pack** | Not public | Only after hosted checkout, server receipt, delivery/reversal and support evidence | A complete versioned result system, not a tiny prompt. |
| **Business / Enterprise** | Not public | Later only after the real team/governance product exists | Not a launch tier. |

**Why no $1 tier:** it cannot carry payment fees, refunds, customer support and
chargeback risk responsibly. The lowest paid entry should be a real outcome
pack or a monthly membership with enough maintained value to retain users.

**Why no $100/$200 self-serve tier now:** high-price tiers must be justified by
real delivered cost or governance. EONAPP does not currently host a high-cost
AI model service, enterprise control plane or support program.

## 3. Referral decision

A subscription percentage, referral commission, revenue share or cash payout
is **not approved**.

- Public wording: **EON Invite**.
- Current behaviour: portable share links and free local cosmetic recognition.
- **No subscription percentage, commission, payout, affiliate income, cash,
  crypto, points, subscription unlock or multi-level reward.**
- This protects users, avoids an incentive programme that can be gamed, and
  keeps the product compatible with an ordinary software/digital-pack merchant
  description.

PayU’s published restricted-business list includes profits or income by
referral of new users, which is a direct reason not to base EONAPP economics
on referral percentage payouts. This does not make normal software billing
impossible, but it makes the former referral-income concept a poor product and
merchant fit.

## 4. Ads, CPA and affiliate choices

**No ads and no CPA/offerwall at launch.**

- Never add ads, sponsored answers, CPA offers, rewarded ads or affiliate
  insertion in EONBOT Chat, Workspace, Vault, Apps, Insights & Forecasts or
  EON City.
- Do not make plans "ad-free"; the whole core product is ad-free.
- Do not use CPA/offerwalls to fund free access. They damage trust, distort
  user behaviour and conflict with the professional work-product position.
- A later clearly-labelled Partner Directory may be evaluated outside core work
  surfaces only after independent policy, disclosure, privacy and partner-fit
  review. It cannot alter EONBOT answers or attach benefits to clicks.

## 5. Payment-processor direction — W450 supersession

1. **Dodo Payments** is the single approval-pending merchant-of-record planning candidate.
   The individual underwriting application is pending; no live processor function
   is connected.
2. **Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU and NOWPayments** are not active candidates and
   must not appear in public purchase paths, provider code, entitlement logic
   or current launch promises.
3. The planned public conversion offer is one transparent **7-day trial** per
   verified customer across the future paid ladder. It stays off until merchant
   approval, approved catalogue/policies, test-mode checkout, signed webhooks,
   idempotency, refund/cancel/dispute handling and human commercial approval
   all pass.
4. Guidance about India AutoPay/Indian-card pending settlement is an account
   verification target only. A checkout return, mandate creation, charge
   initiation or pending state never grants or extends access.

Every future paid transaction must use hosted checkout plus server-side
signature verification, an idempotent receipt/entitlement record, refund and
chargeback reversal, support evidence and a user-account identity. Browser
localStorage can never be a paid-access ledger.

## 6. Institutional Blueprint Collection plan

The product should expand deliberately, not by adding low-quality cards. The
next collection target is **32 free core Blueprints** and **24 future Pro Pack
deliverables**, across six families:

1. Business operations
2. Creator systems
3. Builder and product
4. Research and decisions
5. Events and hospitality
6. Career and personal systems

Every pack must contain: a concrete outcome, required inputs, deliverables,
review checkpoints, local workflow handoff, action classification,
privacy/safety boundary, version number and change notes.

A paid pack must be a maintained multi-deliverable system. No individual
feature, prompt or ordinary local record is eligible for a paywall.
