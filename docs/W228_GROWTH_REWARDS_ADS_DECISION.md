# W228 — Growth, Invite, Reward, Affiliate, and Ads Decision

## Immediate decision: do not turn Share Center into an auto-post or instant-value engine

The current Share Center is correct for launch:

- signed EONAPP / City / Workspace / safe Realm identity links;
- QR/copy/share-card material;
- local AI-assisted campaign drafting;
- user review before every post;
- no social login, OAuth token, webhook, background job, click tracking, automatic posting, payment, credit, commission, or payout.

It is deliberately **not** a public chat-sharing feature.

## Why instant rewards for raw sharing are rejected

A reward for merely copying/posting a link is a poor product mechanism: it is easy to fake, creates spam pressure, attracts multi-account abuse, and turns every user into an undisclosed promoter. A free subscription day, feature unlock, discount, theme, extra generation capacity, badge, or non-transferable utility pass is still something of value. It should not be described as automatically “legal for sure.” It needs a clear program, disclosure, terms, abuse controls, and review for the jurisdictions/platforms actually used.

## Recommended Phase 11: Access Milestones (not “points”)

Do this only after an explicit implementation proposal and review:

**Name:** EON Access Milestones

**What it may grant:**
- temporary private-generation capacity;
- temporary Workspace/project capacity;
- non-transferable City/Realm cosmetics;
- limited beta access;
- a time-bounded feature trial.

**What it must never be:**
- money, cash equivalent, withdrawable balance, token, crypto asset, transferable NFT, resale right, investment return, wage, or passive-income claim.

**Qualifying event:** never a raw click, page view, impression, copied link, generic share, ad view/click, idle time, or self-report. Use a server-verified meaningful event, such as a unique account activation that passes a retention/abuse window or an approved documented contribution.

**Minimum controls before any activation:**
- server-side issuance ledger with `created`, `pending`, `eligible`, `granted`, `reversed`, `rejected`, and `expired` states;
- one reward per verified user/event, anti-self-referral and suspicious-pattern controls;
- explicit caps, expiry, reversal, support appeal, and audit trail;
- clear “benefit for sharing” disclosure template in every campaign draft;
- age/jurisdiction/product-policy decision; privacy minimization and deletion rules;
- no browser-local issuance or bypass path;
- formal legal/tax/consumer/advertising review of the exact final wording and flow.

## Later Phase 12: paid affiliate, only after commerce exists

A future paid affiliate program may be sensible, but it must be entirely separate from the invite program:

`share -> attributed visitor -> verified official EONAPP order -> pending -> eligible -> payable -> paid/reversed`

It must be one level only, official EONAPP items only, server-backed, refund-aware, fraud-reviewed, disclosed, and gated behind merchant/receipt/delivery/support policy. No rate should appear in the UI until it is actually approved and active.

## Ads decision

Do not put ads in Chat, Vault, Local AI, City gameplay, Market generation, or Trade Research. Do not reward ad impressions/clicks.

If advertising is revisited later, use a separate **Sponsored Discovery** page or card area with:
- clear `Sponsored` label;
- no targeting based on Vault/chat secrets;
- no performance/financial promises;
- no crypto/VDA promotions until a dedicated policy, brand-safety, and jurisdiction review exists;
- no ad reward mechanics;
- server-side provider validation and kill switch;
- a dismiss/feedback action and frequency cap.

## Automation decision

The viral feature should be **campaign preparation**, not mass posting. AI may create a differentiated draft, link card, recommended cadence, disclosure line, and channel checklist. The user must approve each publishing action. A later official platform integration needs explicit OAuth scope, per-post/user consent, rate and duplication limits, disclosure tooling, opt-out, audit logs, and each platform’s current developer-policy approval.
