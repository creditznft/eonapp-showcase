# R4-COMM-03 — Solo Pricing, Currency Parity and Catalogue Decision

**Status:** Planning-only source decision. It activates no merchant, checkout,
payment rail, recurring subscription, price screen, paid pack, coupon,
entitlement, referral benefit, advertising, CPA offer, payout, provider SDK or
customer billing flow.

This document **supersedes the price table, tier ladder and organisation-plan
ideas in R4-COMM-02**. The R4-COMM-02 provider-research and EON Invite safety
boundaries remain in force.

## CEO decision

EONAPP will be a generous free local-first product with a simple **solo-user
ladder**. It does not pursue Team, Scale or Enterprise plans in the current
roadmap.

The paid model later sells maintained official outcome systems and their
updates—not basic projects, local storage, safety tools, exports, tiny prompts,
ad-free access, fake AI quotas, financial signals, cloud-backup promises or
human support promises.

## Fixed INR/USD planning book

The price books use a rounded **₹100 per US$ planning anchor**, tested against a
26 June 2026 reference snapshot of ₹94.6895 per US$. This keeps INR and USD
values within roughly six percent of one another after sensible price rounding.

This is **not runtime FX conversion**. Each provider checkout must use an
approved fixed India or global price book and clearly display its own final
currency/tax treatment. The books should be reviewed at most twice a year, or
if a sustained material change affects FX, tax or provider cost.

## What each later solo tier must earn

### EON Free — active now

Free remains genuinely useful: Apps, Workspace, the 32 official free
Blueprints, 16 local approval-first workflows, Insights & Forecasts, local
exports, backup guidance, guest use and safety controls.

### EON Plus — $4.99 / ₹499

A maintained professional Pack library with version and migration notes.

It cannot be offered until there are at least 24 maintained Plus Pack
deliverables, six browser-reviewed workroom systems, a provider-approved
test-mode billing lifecycle and an AI-first self-service exception path for
failed paid access, privacy, security and unresolved billing failures.

### EON Studio — $14.99 / ₹1,499

Plus plus deeper creator, business and research operating systems with reusable
local dashboards and advanced workroom sets.

It cannot sell until the Studio collection has real distinct value beyond Plus
and evidence shows repeat use rather than one-off Pack purchases.

### EON Power — $29.99 / ₹2,999

Studio plus the full current non-Signature single-user Official Pack library
and advanced cross-workroom operating systems.

It cannot sell until at least 36 maintained premium solo Packs exist, all
cross-workroom flows are browser/mobile reviewed, and recurring billing,
cancellation, refund and dispute reversal are proven in test mode.

### EON Max — $49.99 / ₹4,999

The all-access individual catalogue: Power plus current Signature Packs and a
clearly labelled stable-preview Pack stream while the membership is active.

EON Max must not be public until there are at least 50 maintained premium
single-user Packs, a 12-month Pack release/migration cadence and complete
licence/cancellation/renewal/reversal proof. It is **not** Enterprise, a team
plan, priority support, unlimited hosted AI, financial intelligence, cloud
backup or an account-management product.

## Not in the current roadmap

The following are deliberately removed from the current pricing ladder:

- EON Team
- EON Scale
- Enterprise
- per-seat pricing
- organisation roles or SSO promises
- contractual support or human response-time promises

They can be reconsidered only in a future separately governed product after
real shared workspaces, roles, controls, billing administration and accountable
operations exist.

## EON Invite remains a safe customer-promotion design

A future EON Invite can offer a limited single-level coupon or capped access
extension after a genuinely new paid user survives the refund/dispute window.
It remains inactive until the selected provider accepts the exact catalogue and
promotion and server-backed grant/reversal proof exists.

It never becomes cash, commission, subscription-percentage income, revenue
share, affiliate payout, stored value, crypto, token, gift card, multi-level
scheme, reward for a share/click/free signup, or browser-only grant.

## Pricing comparison guardrail

The ladder is intentionally below the current individual/professional bands of
major work products while refusing to copy their cost structure. Current
published examples include ChatGPT Go at $8 and Plus at $20, Notion Plus at
$10 and Business at $20 per member/month, Canva Business at $20 per person per
month, and Zapier Professional at $19.99/month billed annually. EONAPP must
not promise hosted AI capacity, connectors, team controls or human support
that it does not operate merely to imitate those prices.

## Non-negotiable activation order

1. Keep every commercial flag false and do not show these prices publicly.
2. Build and review the first six paid-worthy Packs before any public catalog.
3. Obtain written provider approval for the narrow productivity-Pack catalogue,
   India payout method and EON Invite promotion.
4. Prove one hosted test-mode one-time Pack: signed webhook, idempotent receipt,
   personal licence, refund/dispute reversal and local access reconciliation.
5. Complete W276 update/rollback restoration and Preview/browser/device proof.
6. Consider one-time Pack activation only after all five proofs exist.
7. Consider Plus only after Pack usage shows repeated ongoing value.
8. Treat Studio, Power and Max as unavailable until their respective catalogue
   gates are truthfully met.
