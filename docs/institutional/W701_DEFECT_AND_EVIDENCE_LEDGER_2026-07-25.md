## Immediate institutional blockers

### CITY-P0-001 — Below-world camera exposure

- **Observed:** owner can zoom or orbit below the visible surface and expose the scene underside.
- **Source-confirmed:** the ArcRotate camera had no hard beta, radius, target-height or per-frame position safety authority.
- **W703 acceptance:** hard camera constraints, per-frame enforcement, restored-pose sanitation, and an underside occluder.

### CITY-P0-002 — Unsafe restored and transition poses

- **Observed risk:** landmark focus, exploration restore and destination transitions can directly assign coordinates.
- **Source-confirmed:** several entry points accepted finite coordinates but did not apply one global world boundary.
- **W703 acceptance:** every entry path uses the same transition sanitizer; non-finite or escaped coordinates fail closed.

### STATE-P0-001 — Fragmented foreground work context

- **Observed:** Projects, Atlas, NEXUS and City do not consistently communicate one selected object and one active context.
- **Architectural cause:** multiple projections exist, but there was no small canonical cross-surface contract with a single revision.
- **W702 acceptance:** one immutable state contract, pure reducers, surface-specific projections, no second persistence layer and no private content duplication.

### TRUST-P0-001 — Missing reviewed foreground gateway

- **Risk:** cross-surface navigation and selection can otherwise accumulate inconsistent direct handlers.
- **W702 acceptance:** prepare, review, confirm or reject; internal routes only; no provider, payment, network, microphone, camera or external execution.
