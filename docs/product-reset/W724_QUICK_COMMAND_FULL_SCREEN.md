# W724 — Full-screen Quick Command

The Orb is a small persistent launcher. It is not the workspace.

Opening the Orb now produces one full-screen 2D command surface on desktop, tablet, and mobile. It exposes four stable primary actions—Continue, New, Ask EONBOT, and Share—then page-context jumps, recent local items, status, advanced controls, and Help.

The previous frontend Pulse/Live Nexus is not mounted on ordinary app pages. Its 3D concepts and City station assets are preserved for EON City. City Nexus stations will open the same productive 2D panel framework in W725 and W733.
