## Real-work rule

The renderer may display a terminal, workbench, chair, guide or portal. It does not embed private project text in public textures and does not perform the native operation itself. The terminal opens a review card; a separate explicit control opens the canonical route.

## Density rule

- Lite: at least 6 procedural street lights / furnishing groups.
- Balanced: at least 11.
- Cinematic: at least 16.
- One binary district resident at a time.
- Distant population may use procedural silhouettes.
- Do not duplicate high-poly characters merely to make the city look busy.
- Codex must visually confirm that streets do not feel empty, while preserving frame budget and navigation clearance.

## Realm decision

Realm Studio is a private local identity/design space. It is not a public publishing shortcut and not a financial or marketplace surface. Codex must verify that Realm entry clearly communicates privacy, creativity and return-loop value.
