# W652–W654 EONCITY CEO Scorecard and Decisions

## Product definition locked

**EONCITY is EONAPP’s private spatial operating system: a Command Room, Living Dashboard, truthful Agent Theater and optional 3D world in one product.**

It must be useful in under ten seconds after entry, understandable without a tutorial, enjoyable without fake gamification, and safe without making restrictions the headline.

## CEO decisions

1. **Command Room is the default authenticated landing layer.**
2. **3D Explore is optional and always one click away.**
3. **The guest entrance sells the promise first and explains trust second.**
4. **EONBOT stays available inside City.**
5. **Seven primary work destinations receive visual priority.**
6. **Four private/system destinations use a quieter secondary rack.**
7. **Share and District Map are hero actions, not competing work cards.**
8. **Every destination—including in-City EONBOT and Agent Theater lanes—requires review and a second visible click.**
9. **The signed-out Google CTA is pinned to the reviewed same-origin auth start route.**
10. **Command Room keyboard listeners are lifecycle-owned and cannot multiply across renderer restarts.**
11. **Entertainment comes from place, movement, story, identity and finite authored expeditions—not fake rewards or fake work.**
12. **Agent Theater animates only from real, receipt-backed state.**
13. **City assets remain static, content-addressed Cloudflare Pages files.**
14. **A normal application update does not invalidate unchanged City art.**
15. **No production release before exact-dist Cloudflare Preview, real Google login proof, real-device metrics and owner GO.**

## Remaining blockers

- Human first-impression screenshots and video.
- Real Google-authenticated route and session proof.
- Desktop and mobile-landscape interaction matrix.
- Pathfinder Prime versus Pathfinder A comparison.
- Material, deformation, clipping and foot-contact review.
- FPS, frame-time, memory, context-loss, heat and network evidence.
- True LOD1/LOD2 asset generation and approval.
- Explicit owner production approval.
