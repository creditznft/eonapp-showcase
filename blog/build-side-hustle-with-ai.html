The 60-minute Business Pack workflow

1. Niche Scan (10 min)

Prompt: "Find 5 underserved niches where small operators can sell AI workflows."

2. Offer Generator (10 min)

Build one core offer: who it is for, what result it gives, why it is different.

3. Pricing Strategist (10 min)

Generate 3 price anchors: starter, core, premium. Keep each tied to clear deliverables.

4. Landing Draft (15 min)

Use Build mode for a one-page structure: hook, pains, offer, proof, CTA.

5. Launch Plan (15 min)

Create your first 7-day content + outreach plan. No ads required for initial validation.
