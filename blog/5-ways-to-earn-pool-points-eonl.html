5 Ways to Earn Pool Points and EonLite

Every action you take on EON earns Pool Points. At epoch end, those points determine your share of the EonLite that was minted. Here's how to maximise them.

6 min read · Earning guide · ← Back to blog

How Pool Points work

Pool Points decouple your effort from token price. When EonLite is minted (by the emission controller), 100% of it flows into 5 pool buckets. Each bucket's EonLite is distributed pro-rata based on your share of that pool's accumulated points. The more you contribute to a specific pool, the larger your slice.

Pool Weight Earned by
Platform Pool40%AI missions, tool usage
Creator Pool25%Publishing content, templates, agents
Trade Pool15%Research queries, watchlist activity
Realm Pool12%Referrals, community activity
NFT Pool8%NFT minting, lootbox activity

The 5 fastest earning paths

1 — Platform Pool (40%)

Run AI missions on WorkBench

Each mission run earns 15 Platform points. Batch runs earn 40 points. A completion streak adds a daily bonus. If you run missions a day for a week, you're accumulating 15+ points from this alone. This is the highest-weight pool so it's the best baseline earner.

2 — Creator Pool (25%)

Publish templates and AI agents

Publishing a creator template earns 20 Creator points. Publishing an AI agent earns 25. Even writing content posts earns 10 each. Creator pool has the second-highest weight, so consistent publishing pays well over time.

Open Creator Studio →

3 — Trade Pool (15%)

Run research on Trade

Every AI research query run on Trade earns 10 Trade points. Saving a research piece earns 5. Adding an asset to your watchlist earns more. Trade is also the most useful daily habit — stay informed and earn at the same time.

Open Trade →

4 — Realm Pool (12%)

Refer users and set up your Realm

Setting up your Realm profile earns 50 Realm points once. A successful referral (user signs up via your link) earns 25. When a referred user activates a plan, you earn 100. Referrals are the highest-velocity point earner per action if you have an audience.

Open Realm →

5 — NFT Pool (8%)

Mint and trade NFTs on Market

Minting an NFT earns 50 NFT points. Opening a lootbox earns 5 each. NFT pool is the smallest by weight but the easiest passive earner once your content is listed.

Open Market →

Subscription multipliers

Free plan earns 1x. Spark plan 1.5x. Builder 2x. Pro and Operator 3x. There's also a daily cap per tier to prevent farming abuse: 200/day free, up to 1,500/day on Pro+.

💡 Best daily routine: WorkBench missions (45 pts) + 1 Trade research (10 pts) + 1 Creator post (10 pts) = 65 pts/day. Over 30 days that's 1,950+ points in the pool.

How to see your pool breakdown

Go to Vault → Pool Points & Claims tab. You'll see your breakdown by pool, estimated EonLite allocation, and the on-chain claim button once distributions open.
