Build better market decisions with structure, not noise.

EON Trade helps you turn raw market data into a defendable thesis by combining live market feeds, watchlist context, and AI analysis.

Core workflow

  1. Choose an asset and timeframe.
  2. Generate bull/base/bear case outputs.
  3. Save key assumptions in your research archive.
  4. Add the asset to your watchlist and define trigger levels.
  5. Optionally connect Binance read-only keys for portfolio-aware prompts.

Prompt template

Build a 90-day thesis for [ASSET].
Include bull/base/bear scenarios, key invalidation levels,
macro dependencies, and risk-adjusted position sizing notes.

Safety policy

Trade is advisory only. No automatic execution. Keep your process explicit and always review with your own risk rules.
