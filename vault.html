What to keep separate

Keep the Capsule and passphrase in separate places. A Capsule intentionally excludes API/provider keys, recovery secrets, wallets, OAuth sessions, raw media, model files and unknown storage.

A recovery rehearsal is guidance only. It never creates a backup, restores data or certifies a device.
