# W226 — Phase 9 Commercial Decision Gate

Date: 24 June 2026

## Decision

All commercial systems remain **no-go** and inactive.

- Invite & Share Center links are discovery invitations only.
- Clicks, views, generic sharing, idle activity, ad views, and browser-local attribution create no value.
- Affiliate rate is 0%; there is no active eligibility, reversal window, ledger, payout, or cash-out path.
- Official catalog, checkout, receipt, delivery, refund/dispute, user selling, public Realm storefronts, token programme, transfer, conversion, and mining claims remain off.

## Implemented

- Machine-readable `commercial-decision-gate` registry and terminal blocked activation response.
- Billing status panel that states the decision boundary rather than implying that any commercial programme exists.
- Contract record describing written go/no-go requirements.
- Unit/browser tests and release-chain inclusion.

## Required before a future written go decision

1. Official merchant/publisher, catalog, order, receipt, delivery, reversal, support, refund/dispute, reconciliation, and production proof.
2. One-level only server-side affiliate policy with fraud/reversal controls, disclosure, eligibility, maximum rate, tax/payout policy, and appeals.
3. Independent token contract/role/treasury evidence plus specialist review before any EON Lite claim.
4. Fresh permitted-browser production proof and rollback plan.
