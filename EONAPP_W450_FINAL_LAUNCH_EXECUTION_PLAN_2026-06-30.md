**Date:** 1 July 2026  
**Current source baseline:** W448 validated checkpoint + W449–W453.1 production cleanup/proof tooling + W450.1 Dodo catalogue envelope + W452.1–W452.2 active-route cleanup + W455.1 world-composition + W456.1 guide-cast + W457.1 mobile/share evidence packet + W458.1 Sync status-proof + W459.1 redacted recovery rehearsal + W460.1 current local job-receipt Activity Center bridge + W461.1 Telegram/Research public-edge proof runner + W462.1 trust/accessibility source audit + W466.1 fail-closed production evidence board + W467.1 redaction-safe Codex deployment handoff  
**Launch definition:** a local-first AI workspace with a premium original EON Noir City, owner-confirmed identity, explicit user-controlled Sync Basic, privacy-safe sharing and one approved hosted subscription checkout.

---

## 1. Locked product boundary

EONAPP launches with a generous free local-first core. The public product is:

- EONBOT Chat, Projects, Library, Workspace, Apps and EON Forge;
- local-first tools, BYOK/provider setup and encrypted backup/recovery controls;
- EON City as an original visual workspace, not a second automation engine;
- Research Lab at `/insights`, with no live prices, broker, execution, prediction, stake or financial-advice claim;
- manual privacy-safe sharing and optional Telegram onboarding/help/updates/deep links;
- a future single-provider hosted subscription and product-licence lane only after approval and proof.

The following remain out of launch scope: ads, rewarded ads, offerwalls, Telegram rewards, sponsor-credit unlocks, trading execution, prediction-market stakes, crypto/tokens/wallets, NFT resale/marketplace, referral payouts or discounts, browser push, OAuth social posting, automatic external actions, cloud Vault/secret custody, and client-side payment entitlement.

## 4. Dodo payment decision

Dodo Payments is the single planned billing provider. The application is in individual merchant underwriting. This does **not** activate a payment flow.

The launch implementation will use hosted checkout and a small isolated entitlement service. It will never accept raw card data in EONAPP, never store private work in billing, never grant access from a browser redirect and never create a subscription/trial from client-only state.

Free trials are planned as an aggressive but transparent conversion mechanism: **one 7-day trial per verified customer across the paid ladder**, no plan hopping, provider-hosted consent required, exact price/renewal/cancellation shown before checkout, and server-side entitlement only. The public trial remains off until W463–W465 pass. Guidance received during onboarding about India mandate timing is recorded as unverified until it is confirmed inside the approved merchant account and test lifecycle.
